1. Buttons and Shortcuts

This guide is for Xuesu 3 readers running SnowMux community firmware. Insert the SD card and choose your language at startup. You can use this guide to practice turning pages.

  • Up and Down: move through lists or turn pages while reading.
  • Press OK: open the selected item, or open the reader menu while reading.
  • Hold OK: go back or cancel by default.

On screens with a main tab bar, hold OK to return focus to the bar. If you are in a folder, back out of it first. Press Up or Down to switch tabs, then press OK to enter.

Press OK Twice to Open the Shortcut Panel

On a main tab or reading page, press OK twice in quick succession (about 0.3 seconds apart or less). In the pop-up panel, use Up and Down to move, press OK to select, or hold OK to close it. The common shortcuts are Sleep, Night Mode, Refresh Screen, and Reading Orientation.

While reading, press OK twice to open the panel, then press OK once to show or hide the status bar and return to the text. The device remembers your choice.

When the current screen has an extra action, it appears first in the panel. You can also press OK twice in these places:

  • Files and Library: select an item to access File Actions or Library Actions. The library sort tabs also offer Library Actions.
  • Bookmarks and clippings: select an item to access Bookmark Actions or Delete Clipping.
  • Recents and reading stats: select a book to remove its record. The book file stays on the card.
  • OPDS catalogs with multiple pages: select Catalog pages. The menu lists the previous, next, first, or last pages the server provides.
  • Standby: select Set as sleep screen to save the current clock face.

When a saved Wi-Fi network, installed plugin, or Bluetooth page turner mapping is selected, press OK twice for the option to forget, uninstall, or clear it. Screens such as the reader toolbar and word picker have their own controls; follow the hints shown there.

2. Reading and Text Layout

Choose a book in Library or Files and press OK. By default, Up goes to the previous page and Down goes to the next. You can reverse them in Settings > Controls > Side Button Layout (Reader).

Press OK while reading to find chapters, text settings, bookmarks, or reading progress. The menu layout varies by theme. When Page Footnotes appears in the EPUB list menu, select it to see the footnotes on the current page. Hold OK to return to the text.

EPUB shortcuts you can set: In Settings > Controls > Long-Press Button Behavior, set holding a page button to skip chapters or change orientation. Long-Press Menu can set holding OK in a book to bookmark, look up a word, create a clipping, or sync. Disable it to restore the default Back action.

Change the font, size, line spacing, or margins from the reader menu or Settings > Reader > Text Settings. Press Up at the first row or Down at the last row to return focus to the tab bar. Use Up and Down to switch tabs and press OK to enter the selected tab.

Reader settings also let you manage fonts and offline dictionaries. The reader may need to paginate a book when you first open it or after you change the text layout.

3. Adding Books

USB Transfer

Open Apps > File Transfer > USB Drive and connect the reader to a computer with a USB data cable. Copy books to the SD card drive, for example into /Books/. Safely eject the drive on the computer before opening books on the reader again.

Wi-Fi Transfer

Under File Transfer, choose Join a Network or Create Hotspot. Follow the on-screen steps, then open the address shown on the reader in a phone or computer browser. The transfer page has no sign-in. Use it on a trusted network and exit when finished.

Common formats include EPUB, TXT, and XTC/XTCH. SnowMux can also open unencrypted MOBI and AZW3 files up to 4 MiB, as well as DOCX. DOCX text and supported inline images are reflowed, so complex pages may look different from Word.

4. Wi-Fi and Phone Setup

To connect to your router, open Settings > Connectivity > Wi-Fi Setup. Scan the QR code to join the temporary hotspot or press either side button to see its password. If the setup page does not open automatically, enter the address shown on the reader in your phone browser.

Select your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, enter its password, and submit. Wait for the reader to confirm the connection. It can reuse this network the next time a feature needs Wi-Fi.

SnowMux uses similar phone forms for OPDS server settings, CrossPoint Sync (KOReader) credentials and addresses, catalog search, and book unlock phrases. Follow the prompts to join the hotspot and submit the form, then press OK on the reader to continue. Hold OK to leave an unsubmitted form.

5. Library and Factory Reading History

If a new book appears in Files but not in Library, select an existing book or focus the sort tabs. Press OK twice and choose Library Actions > Refresh library. In an unsorted library, press OK twice from the list.

Select a file and press OK twice. Use File Actions to open, move, or delete it. Check the title before confirming a deletion.

With the SD card you used in the factory firmware, open Settings > System > Import factory reading history. Books still on the card are added to your recent list. If you have already read a book in SnowMux, your existing position takes priority. Otherwise the reader tries to open it at the percentage saved by the factory firmware.

6. Apps and Sleep Screens

Which Apps Appear

A fresh configuration shows Library, File Transfer, WeRead, AirPage, Reading Statistics, and Standby. OPDS Browser appears after you add an OPDS server.

Ugly Avatar, Buddy, and Electronic Woodfish are hidden by default. Open Settings > System > App Visibility. Select an app and press OK to set it to On, then hold OK to leave and save.

Sudoku, Gomoku, Sokoban, Chinese Chess, Minesweeper, 2048, Pixel Switch, and Calculator require touch or separate Left and Right buttons. Their entries are unavailable on Xuesu 3.

Sleep and Clock Faces

Press OK twice and select Sleep, or choose Settings > System > Sleep. Press OK to wake the reader. Choose Settings > Display > Time to Sleep to set the idle timeout.

Open Apps > Standby and tap Up or Down to preview Sloppy Clock, Chinese Calendar, Month Grid, Newspaper, and National Day Fireworks. Press OK to invert the colors or hold OK to exit. Press OK twice and choose Set as sleep screen, or pick a face in Settings > Display > Sleep Screen.

Set the clock first under Settings > System > Date & Time. Sloppy Clock and the three factory-style clock faces update by the minute in sleep. Chinese Calendar updates when the local date changes.

7. Updating SnowMux

If your build offers Settings > System > Check for Updates, follow the prompts to update over Wi-Fi. If there is no update menu, visit the SnowMux installer from a computer: clown145.github.io/snowmux/.

Keep the full backup downloaded when you first installed SnowMux. An on-device update may overwrite the slot that contains the factory firmware; read the confirmation before proceeding. Keep the reader charged and avoid interrupting power or restarting it during the update.

If the update menu offers Restore factory firmware, follow its prompts to download and install the factory app. Use the original Flash backup to restore the entire device.

8. Troubleshooting

Ghosting on the screen: Press OK twice and choose Refresh Screen. You can also set the full refresh interval in Settings > Display > Refresh Frequency.

A book will not open or has missing letters: Check that it was copied completely and is in a supported format. If only some characters are blank, try a font that includes them.

The phone cannot open the setup page: Make sure it is still connected to the reader's temporary hotspot. If asked, stay connected despite the lack of internet access. Enter the address shown on the reader in your phone browser.

SD card errors or slow transfers: Back up your books and /.crosspoint/ before checking or replacing the card. That folder holds reading progress and settings.
